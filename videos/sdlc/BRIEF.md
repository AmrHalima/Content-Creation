## Intent

A 60–90s bilingual-package teaser video about the Software Development Life Cycle for
people who already know the diagram. The tension to solve: sophisticated audience, very
short runtime — solved with an opinionated angle, not compression of a beginner script.
Tone: peer-to-peer senior-engineer talk, dense, wry, zero hand-holding. Fast, hook-driven,
high information per second.

Thesis: the seven phases are the least interesting part of SDLC. The real content is
(a) where each methodology lets reality revise the plan — Waterfall: through a change
request; Agile: every sprint; DevOps: several times a day — and (b) that failures happen
at the handoffs between phases, never inside one.

English is the master. An Egyptian colloquial Arabic (عامية مصرية) variant is authored
fully — script, on-screen text, RTL-aware composition — but NOT rendered without the
user hearing the Arabic TTS voice first.
