## Line 1 — Hook (Frame 1)

**Time:** estimate 8s (real VO wins)
**Delivery:** Second sentence a touch faster. 'in a row' lands as the boxes arrive.

    A string isn't one thing. It's a lot of tiny things in a row.

## Line 2 — Concept A (Frame 2)

**Time:** estimate 7s (real VO wins)
**Delivery:** Flat and quiet: this is the smallest thing in the part, so the voice goes calm, not careful.

    A character is one symbol. One letter, one digit, even one space.

## Line 3 — Concept B (Frame 3)

**Time:** estimate 14s (real VO wins)
**Delivery:** 'counting from zero' is the keyword of the part. The last sentence is correction #3: said plainly, and the bracket is already on screen when it lands.

    Line those symbols up and you have a string. Each one sits at a position, counting from zero. A slice takes the letters before its end.

## Line 4 — The example (Frame 4)

**Time:** estimate 8.5s (real VO wins)
**Delivery:** Slow on 'od'. Three short sentences, no room for a fourth: the code and the bracket do the rest.

    Word is code. Position zero gives you c. Slice one to three gives you od.

## Line 5 — The save (Frame 5)

**Time:** estimate 7.9s (real VO wins)
**Delivery:** Instruction, not encouragement. No lift at the end.

    Save this. Then slice your own name. Position zero is the first letter.
