## Constraint audit (run against this script before any TTS was spent)

- **Contrast-reveal ban:** no "it's not X, it's Y" anywhere. Frame 6 states the thesis
  directly. The one negation, "The machines in between just forward", is affirmative in
  sense (they forward, and that is all they do).
- **Em-dash ban on short copy:** none. Not in narration, not in on-screen text.
- **Triad cap:** exactly one, Frame 6 ("One layer, one partner, all at once").
- **No mnemonics:** none, and none teased.
- **Scoped postal image:** the post office appears in Frame 5 only, for Layer 3
  forwarding, and is never reused for the stack or for Layer 7.
- **Zero references between parts:** no "part", no "as we saw", no "next time I…".
  "Next time you see the diagram" points at the viewer's own life.
- **Verbatim hook (ruling 5):** Line 1 is untouched.
- **Sideways frame established:** this part is the thesis carrier, so the rule applies to it
  differently from parts 2 and 3. Line 1 puts "the stack" and "the wrong direction" on the
  table together; line 2 performs the wrong reading; line 3 flips it. The flip is audible by
  ~14s of a ~60s film, which is as early as it can go without skipping the counter-reading
  the flip needs to exist against.
