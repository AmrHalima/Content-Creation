## Line 1 — Hook (Frame 1)

**Delivery:** Deadpan. The screen shows the quotes; the voice deliberately does not.

    Why does five plus five give you fifty-five?

## Line 2 — The type decides (Frame 2)

**Delivery:** One breath, no comma pause. "carries a type" is the keyword.

    Because every value carries a type, and the type decides what the operation means.

## Line 3 — The four (Frame 3)

**Delivery:** Even pace, no list-voice; this is the one enumeration in the part and it is the
subject itself.

    Four you will use every day. Int for whole numbers, float for decimals, str for text, and bool
    for true or false.

## Line 4 — The example (Frame 4)

**Delivery:** Plain. Then a beat before "It glues", two words, alone.

    Five plus five gives ten. Put them both in quotes, and plus stops adding. It glues.

## Line 5 — The quiz (Frame 5)

**Delivery:** "star" for `*`, never "multiply". Flat instruction at the end.

    So here is the quiz. Quote five, star three. What does Python give you? Comment it.
